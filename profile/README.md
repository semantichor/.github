**Words cross. Thinking does not.**

When your work moves to a new session or another model, the text comes with you. The structure does not: what was settled, what was still open, what was only a proposal, where each claim came from, what you still owe.

The next session reads everything with equal weight. Questions left open on purpose quietly close. Obligations disappear without being refused or deferred. Sources blur.

Memory, as it works today, carries content. It does not decide whether the work may go on.

SSCAR does. Five agents with strictly bounded authority, plus an integrator. The work goes on only when every condition holds at once. If anything is missing, SSCAR stops, says why, and waits for you. The last word is yours.

It keeps defects, open questions and obligations apart. The record stays on your computer. Its work can be checked again, offline. It works with any AI that can work with your files. A change of model is checked at the gate, not absorbed.

**Continuity is not stored. It is decided.**

SSCAR, Session State Continuity & Anchor Replay, is published as a complete specification. The repositories below hold it.
